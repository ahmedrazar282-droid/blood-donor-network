How it works

Three simple steps, from registration to a fulfilled request.

1

Register as a donor

Add your blood group, city and availability. You stay in control and can switch availability off at any time.

2

Post or receive a request

Families post a request in a minute. We match compatible, available donors, and the same city comes first.

3

Donate and close the request

Donors accept, share their contact only after saying yes, and the request is marked fulfilled when the need is met.
